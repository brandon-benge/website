Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · Benge Software LLC

AI CAD ("the app") is published by Benge Software LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and, importantly, that we do not operate a backend server that receives, stores, or processes your projects or personal data. The app is local-first: your project source lives on your device and, if you choose to connect it, in a GitHub repository you own.

What stays on your device

Your project files, app settings, and any credentials you enter — your GitHub authorization and any AI provider API key — are stored only in your device's local storage and Keychain. We never receive, transmit to our own servers, or store a copy of these credentials.

What you choose to connect

GitHub

If you authorize AI CAD's GitHub App, the app talks directly to GitHub's API using your authorization. Your project source, revision history, and any published preview site are stored in your own GitHub repository, governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Your AI provider

AI CAD does not run its own AI service. When you ask AI CAD to generate or change a design, the relevant project content and your request are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you've configured with your own API key — for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, xAI, DeepInfra, Mistral, or OpenCode. That exchange is governed by the privacy policy of the provider you chose, not by us. We recommend reviewing your chosen provider's policy before connecting it.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, your project metadata is synced across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, under Apple's privacy policy. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, not on servers we operate.

What we (Benge Software LLC) collect

Because AI CAD has no first-party backend, we do not collect, receive, or store your project content, credentials, or usage data on our own infrastructure. Standard, aggregated App Store analytics provided by Apple (such as install counts and crash reports, if you opt in) may be available to us through App Store Connect, subject to Apple's privacy policy. We do not sell personal data, because we do not hold any to sell.

Children's privacy

AI CAD is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out from the Support page.
