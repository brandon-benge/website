Good to know

AI CAD uses AI to propose design changes and can, at your direction, push those changes to your repository or permanently delete a project. A couple of things worth keeping in mind:

  • AI-generated changes are proposals, not guarantees — review a preview before you accept or push a change.
  • Destructive actions, like deleting a project repository, always require your explicit confirmation and cannot be undone by us afterward.
